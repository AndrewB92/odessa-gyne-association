  ## About the conference

  The scientific and practical conference **Endometriosis: Multidisciplinary Aspects of Patient Management** took place on 15 March 2024 in a hybrid format. More than 250 participants from Odesa, Mykolaiv and other regions of Ukraine joined the event.

  The programme brought together obstetrician-gynecologists, urologists, surgeons, gynecologic oncologists, proctologists, healthcare managers and university staff. It addressed genital and extragenital endometriosis, fertility treatment, urinary and bowel involvement, surgical and hormonal management, international clinical recommendations and complex case discussions.

  The conference resolution emphasised evidence-based care, continuity between specialties, multidisciplinary case review, preventive examinations and continued CPD education. The archive also includes the assessment and video recording.
