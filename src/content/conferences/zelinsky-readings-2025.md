  ## About the conference

  The fourth interdisciplinary scientific and practical conference with international participation in the Professor Oleksandr Zelinsky Readings series took place in Odesa on 9–10 May 2025 in a hybrid format.

  The programme covered maternal and child healthcare organisation, pregnancy planning, antenatal and intrapartum care, pregnancy complications, emergency obstetrics, perinatal and neonatal infections, reproductive medicine, endocrinology, gynecology, gynecologic oncology, family medicine and medical genetics. The event also included a round table on higher medical education during crisis conditions and a presentation competition for young researchers.

  Participants could receive CPD points after completing the assessment. The archived source materials preserve the registration conditions, abstract requirements, payment details, programme, stream playlist, testing link and conference resolution.
