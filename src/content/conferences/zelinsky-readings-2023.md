  ## About the conference

  The second interdisciplinary scientific and practical conference with international participation, **Professor Oleksandr Zelinsky Readings: Perinatal and Reproductive Medicine through the “4P” Healthcare Concept**, took place on 28–29 April 2023 in Odesa in an online/hybrid format.

  The conference addressed reproductive health through personalised, predictive, preventive and participatory medicine. It included a young researchers’ competition and masterclasses, with CPD points available after assessment. The archived source preserves registration rules, participation fees, abstract requirements, contact information and the event information card.
