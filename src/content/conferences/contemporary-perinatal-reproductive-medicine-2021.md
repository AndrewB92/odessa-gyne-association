  ## About the conference

  The international interdisciplinary scientific and practical conference **Modern Directions in Perinatal and Reproductive Medicine: From Innovative Research Theory to Practice** took place online on 16–17 April 2021.

  The event was dedicated to Professor Oleksandr Zelinsky, a prominent Ukrainian obstetrician-gynecologist, researcher, teacher and one of the initiators of the Association of Obstetricians and Gynecologists of Ukraine.

  The programme covered reproductive and general health, prevention of maternal and perinatal morbidity and mortality, obstetrics, gynecology, family medicine, rehabilitation, reproductive endocrinology, COVID-19, modern surgery and a young researchers’ competition.

  ## Main topics

  - Obstetric sepsis and emergency care
  - Vaginal birth and reduction of caesarean section
  - Cervical screening
  - Robson classification
  - Reproductive health after COVID-19
  - Pregnancy loss
  - Placental disease and obstetric haemorrhage
  - Vaginal microbiome and P4 medicine
  - Reproductive endocrinology
  - Endovascular and endoscopic technologies
  - Perinatal psychology
  - Young researchers’ programme

  ## In memory of Professor Oleksandr Zelinsky

  Professor Zelinsky developed scientific schools in gynecologic endocrinology, perinatology, pediatric and adolescent gynecology, menopause, reconstructive surgery and reproductive medicine.
