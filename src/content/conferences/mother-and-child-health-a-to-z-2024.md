  ## About the conference

  The international interdisciplinary scientific and practical conference **Mother and Child Health from A to Z: Targeted and Interdisciplinary Issues** took place on 29–30 November 2024. It was organised by Odesa National Medical University with the Odesa Branch of the Association of Obstetricians and Gynecologists of Ukraine as co-organiser.

  The hybrid programme addressed women’s, maternal and newborn health and involved obstetrician-gynecologists, surgeons, urologists, anesthesiologists, diagnostic specialists, oncologists, neonatologists, pediatricians, therapists and family physicians. The event was listed in the Ministry of Health CPD programme under event number 3708084.

  The source materials include the detailed announcement, registration conditions, abstract requirements and the official programme. The organisers also stated that the main part of the registration proceeds would support medical supplies for a frontline hospital.
