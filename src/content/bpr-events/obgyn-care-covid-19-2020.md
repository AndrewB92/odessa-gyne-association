  ## Programme

  The online professional school-seminar **Obstetric and Gynecological Care during the New COVID-19 Infection** took place on 19 November 2020.

  The programme covered obstetric and gynecological care during the pandemic, maternal mortality cases in the Odesa region, severe COVID-19 in pregnancy, international evidence, intensive-care management of pregnant and postpartum patients, regional clinical and epidemiological experience and patient routing in Odesa.
