  ## About the school

  The professional school **Infectious Diseases in Obstetrics and Gynecology: Today’s Challenges and Solutions** was held in Odesa in a hybrid format in December 2025. The final resolution and assessment materials indicate 18–19 December, while the initial announcement stated 19–20 December; the migrated schedule follows the post-event materials and the discrepancy is documented in the migration report.

  The school addressed obstetric, gynecological, neonatal and perinatal infections, infection control in healthcare facilities, antimicrobial resistance, septic obstetric complications, vaccination and interdisciplinary patient management. Participants could receive 13 CPD points for two days after achieving at least 70% in the assessment.

  The archive includes the announcement, programme, assessment and final resolution.
