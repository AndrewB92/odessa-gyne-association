  ## About the professional school

  The nationwide professional school **Diagnostic and Treatment Approaches to Preserving Women’s Health from Birth to Maturity** took place online on 24 November 2022.

  The programme addressed gynecologic oncology, cervical pathology, urogynecology, pregnancy complications, miscarriage, menopausal hormone therapy and stress-related abnormal uterine bleeding. The event carried 10 CPD points; the historical event number was 1008691.
