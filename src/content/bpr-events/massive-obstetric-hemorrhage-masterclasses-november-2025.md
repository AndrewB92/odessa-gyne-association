  ## About the masterclasses

  This workplace-based masterclass series on the prevention and treatment of massive obstetric hemorrhage took place on 5–6 November 2025 at Maternity Hospital No. 1 / City Clinical Hospital No. 11 and the Odesa Regional Perinatal Centre.

  Clinical teams of obstetrician-gynecologists, anesthesiologists, midwives and anesthesia nurses practised emergency teamwork, staged uterine devascularisation, uterine balloon tamponade and hemostatic compression sutures using simulation models. The event carried 8 CPD points.

  The combined archive contains programmes for both dates, registration forms, assessments and two video reports. The post-event report notes that certificates were approved and sent to the email addresses supplied during registration.
